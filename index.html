Why AI Art is a Problem

AI images are frustrating and problematic. If you've been an artist for a long time or often watch videos by SamDoesArt, you already know exactly why they're so troubling.

AI "art" is problematic because it steals bits of other artists' work without their consent and combines them without any real understanding or meaning.

Real art is meant to take time and effort, and it actually has meaning behind it. It's the result of skill, creativity, and personal expression.

How to Spot AI Art

Firstly, you need to know what AI art looks like. It often has unnecessary details and practically only uses very saturated colors.

But a major giveaway is the hands. AI just can't understand what a hand is—they often have too many fingers or just don't look quite right.

Now that you know how to spot AI, let's talk about what you can do to avoid it.

Platforms with AI Filtering

If you do art frequently, you've likely used Pinterest to find references. An incredibly useful feature is the ability to turn off AI images.

To do this on Pinterest: go to Profile Settings → Refine Recommendations → AI Content → Turn Everything Off

Unfortunately, I couldn't find many other websites with AI blocking, but whenever you see AI art, make sure to dislike or downvote it.
